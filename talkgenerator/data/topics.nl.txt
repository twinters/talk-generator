# Topics for talks in Dutch (nl): things a talk can be about, for suggestions (such as talk-generator-web's random
# topic button when a visitor picks Dutch) and for checking talkgenerator on varied topics in Dutch.
# Made by adapting topics.txt where a topic says well in Dutch (in the words a speaker would shout at an improv show,
# not word for word), plus about a third from the Netherlands' and Flanders' own life: food, habits, places,
# weather, holidays, sports and oddly specific situations, all harmless.
# Standard Dutch for both countries; where they say a thing differently, either word (now and then both).
# One topic a line, mostly short (a phone shows about 20 characters of the site's topic field); blank lines,
# comments and repeats are skipped.
