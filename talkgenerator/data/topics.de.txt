# Topics for talks in German (de): things a talk can be about, for suggestions (such as talk-generator-web's random
# topic button, for a talk in German) and for checks on varied topics (talkgenerator check --random 20 --language de).
# About two thirds are adapted from topics.txt where a topic says well in German, in the words a German speaker would
# call out at an improv show, never translated word for word; about a third come from the German-speaking world's own
# life (Germany, Austria and Switzerland: food, habits, places, weather, holidays, school and work). One topic a line,
# mostly short (a phone shows about 20 characters of the site's topic field), nouns capitalised, with an article where
# a German speaker would say one; blank lines, comments and repeats are skipped. All harmless, as in topics.txt.
